03

MILP optimisation results

A mixed-integer linear program (Brouer et al., 2013) decides the speed on every leg, which ports to call or skip, and whether to pay for faster cargo handling, so that the total cost is as small as possible.

Objective function and constraints

In plain words

Minimise fuel + cargo delay + delay carried to the next loop + rollover of skipped cargo + extra handling.

Subject to:

  1. One continuous route: every port called is entered once and left once.
  2. Each leg is sailed between the minimum and maximum speed.
  3. Arrival comes after the previous departure plus the sailing time.
  4. Berthing starts only once the ship has arrived and the berth is open.
  5. Berth time can be cut by at most 20%, only at ports that are called.
  6. Lateness is measured against the planned berth time.
  7. Los Angeles is always called; at most two ports may be skipped in a row.
